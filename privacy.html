AYLA GYNO — Privacy policy

Version 1.0.0 · 27 October 2026

This standard offline edition does not require an account or subscription. Questions, imported images, bookmarks, notes, answers, and exam history are stored locally in this browser’s application storage.

Network use

The browser downloads the application files from the hosting provider and caches them for offline use. The application does not upload your question bank or study progress to an application server. The hosting provider may keep ordinary access logs under its own privacy policy.

No analytics services or advertising networks are integrated. External links, when opened, are subject to the destination website’s policy.

Exported files and backups

Exports and backups are saved to files you choose to download or share. A full backup may contain personal study progress and notes. Keep your backups private and share only the intended content package with students.

Your local data

Progress is not synchronised between devices. Clearing browser or app data, changing the site origin, or uninstalling the app may remove locally saved data. Export a backup before changing devices or hosting addresses. Removing the site’s stored data in browser settings deletes its local application data.

The administrator’s local editing lock does not provide a hosted account system or secure server-side access control. Only publish question content you are authorised to distribute.

For privacy questions, contact alzuhairyabd@gmail.com. Do not send passwords or sensitive patient information.
